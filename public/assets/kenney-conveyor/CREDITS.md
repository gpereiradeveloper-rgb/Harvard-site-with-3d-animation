# Kenney Conveyor Kit

Original GLB meshes by Kenney, CC0 1.0, downloaded 13 November 2026 from https://kenney-assets.itch.io/conveyor-kit . The publisher labels the pack as made without generative AI. Scanner and conveyor geometry is retained; materials and proportions are adapted for Harvard receiving. Decorative trim, scan light, readout, and logistics software are original scene additions. No Vedant Hegde or Fancy proprietary files are included.
