# Ready collection models

Downloaded from the official Poly Haven API on 12 November 2026. Both models
and their original 1K texture maps are released under **CC0 1.0**:
https://polyhaven.com/license and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

- **Desk Lamp Arm 01** — modeling/texturing by Kuutti Siitonen, rigging by Yann Kervran.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/desk_lamp_arm_01
  Official file manifest: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/desk_lamp_arm_01
- **Painted Wooden Chair 01** — Kuutti Siitonen.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_chair_01
  Official file manifest: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/painted_wooden_chair_01
- **Painted Wooden Table** — Kirill Sannikov.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_table
  Official file manifest: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/painted_wooden_table

`ReadyBookSet` shares the existing 2K **Book Encyclopedia Set 01** by John
Malcolm in `../ready-models/encyclopedias/`; see that collection's credits and
manifest. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/book_encyclopedia_set_01 (CC0).

The original glTF geometry, materials, UVs and JPEG texture maps were repackaged
into self-contained GLB files without resampling or replacing their material
maps. All downloaded source files were checked against the API's MD5 hashes.
`manifest.json` records source URLs/hashes and final GLB SHA-256 hashes.

The web component scales and places copies; the models are ready-made assets,
not generated geometric approximations. No association with or endorsement by
Harvard University or the original model authors is implied.

## Library delivery truck

**7.5 t class box truck with roll-up door** — original unbranded model by
Innerscene, released **CC0 1.0** on its official library page:
https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/7-5-t-class-box-truck-with-roll-up-door-12baea66

Official public Download GLB resolved to:
https://files.innerscene.com/library/7-5-t-class-box-truck-with-roll-up-door-12baea66/286c7a3a7174b81d13ed/7-5-t-class-box-truck-with-roll-up-door.glb

The original 6,063,296-byte GLB is preserved unchanged in
`../ready-models/box-truck.glb` (the duplicate download in this folder was
removed). All 157,848 triangles,
UVs, texture maps and physical materials (including curved transmissive glass)
remain. The component batches rigid parts by material and retains two real axle
pivots for wheel rolling. Source dimensions: 7.39 m long × 2.734 m wide ×
3.307 m high. A uniform 4.30 m length gives 1.591 m width and 1.924 m height.
The model has no manufacturer brand or original door animations; no Ford,
Mercedes, Scania or other manufacturer identity is claimed.
