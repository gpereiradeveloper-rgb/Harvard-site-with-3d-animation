# Warehouse handling — ready original CC0 models

Downloaded from the official publishers on 12 November 2026. Original GLBs are
preserved unchanged; only rigid parts are batched at runtime. Provenance,
resolved public download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `manifest.json`.

## Innerscene — original unbranded models, CC0 1.0

- Electric counterbalance forklift:
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/electric-counterbalance-forklift-7acc0547
- Manual pallet jack:
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/manual-pallet-jack-5ff28a4a
- Pallet racking bay with pallets:
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/pallet-racking-bay-with-pallets-821e591f
- Scan Grip barcode scanner:
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/scan-grip-barcode-scanner-fc41ed60

The publisher explicitly dedicates these original assets to CC0 on each page:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

The pallet copied onto the moving equipment is extracted intact from one of the
original racking model's pallets. Its original geometry, UVs and materials are
retained. The source forklift carriage/forks and wheel axles are separate rigid
parts; the component moves those parts, not a replacement geometric vehicle.
The source hydraulic simulation is simplified; the animation is an artistic
scene, not an engineering load or stability certification.

## Poly Haven — Cardboard Box 01, Rahul Chaudhary, CC0 1.0

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/cardboard_box_01
Official file list: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/cardboard_box_01
License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Original 1K photographic PBR maps and geometry are packed into a self-contained
GLB without resampling. Every original download was verified against its MD5 in
the official API. The same full-detail model is instanced for persistent cases.

The two adult operators reuse the MakeHuman-derived CC0 people already credited
in `../ready-models/CREDITS.md`; no duplicate character files were downloaded.

Floor, scanner support frame, lane markings and the workflow are original scene
designs. No manufacturer logo or proprietary Sendoso/Fulfyld model is copied.
