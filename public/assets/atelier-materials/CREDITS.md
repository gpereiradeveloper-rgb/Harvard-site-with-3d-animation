# Archive material photography

Original CC0/public-domain texture maps downloaded from Poly Haven. No live API is called by the website.

- Wood Floor, photographed by Rob Tuytel. https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor — diffuse 4096×4096, OpenGL normal and roughness 2048×2048. Source metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/wood_floor
- White Plaster 02, photographed by Rob Tuytel. https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02 — diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness 2048×2048. Source metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/white_plaster_02
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license — CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The materials are tiled in world units and tinted in the renderer. The architecture is an original Harvard-inspired conceptual library, not a photogrammetric model of an identified Harvard corridor.
