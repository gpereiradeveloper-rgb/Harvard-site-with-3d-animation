## JanSport School Backpack Blue Streak — Google LLC

- File: `jansport-backpack.glb`
- Dataset: Google Scanned Objects, Copyright 2020 Google LLC.
- Original object: https://app.gazebosim.org/GoogleResearch/fuel/models/Jansport_School_Backpack_Blue_Streak
- Primary dataset description and license: https://research.google/blog/scanned-objects-by-google-research-a-dataset-of-3d-scanned-common-household-items/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Adaptations: GLB packaging; uniform fit to 40 cm height; centered and rotated to face away from the wearer; fabric roughness adjustment. The scanned silhouette, pockets, shoulder straps and texture remain.
- Runtime: reading students leave the same persistent backpack on the floor beside their own chair during library errands. It stays at that scene position while its student walks or turns, so the mesh does not pass through the backrest or glide across the floor. A rest-space spine socket is available for an actor who actually wears the bag.
